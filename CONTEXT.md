# Bobby Nuts

Children's stories about Bobby Nuts, a red squirrel moving from the top of our valley down to our house. The stories are drawn from real red squirrel watching in the valley.

## The series

**Bobby Nuts** (series):
The name of the book series and the website, shared with the squirrel.

**Story**:
One short adventure of 400 to 600 words, set at one stretch of Good Wood, in which Bobby Nuts makes one new friend. A Story is also one chapter of a Book.

**Book**:
A first chapter book in the series, made of 4 to 6 Stories in journey order.
_Avoid_: volume, collection

**Moment**:
Something real the family saw or did in Good Wood, written down as raw material for a story.
_Avoid_: idea, note, sighting (a sighting is one kind of Moment)

## Characters

**Bobby Nuts**:
The fictional red squirrel at the heart of every story. He's a mix of the real red squirrels the family has seen from the Squirrel Hide, not any one of them. Bouncy, childlike, very excitable and sweet. Always called by his full name, so he's never mixed up with Bobbi the dog.
_Avoid_: Bobby, Bobby Nut

**Champion**:
A real horse in the Horse Fields, named by the family, who think of him as almost their own. In the stories, the big gentle friend who carries Bobby Nuts across the open field.

**The Nut Box Squirrel**:
Another red squirrel who has been secretly eating from the Nut Boxes. The family ask Bobby Nuts to solve this mystery, and the answer is the friend of his own kind he finds at the end.

## The family

The real family who live at the house in Good Wood. They know Bobby Nuts exists, can talk with him when they're quiet enough, and help him on his journey, and he helps them in return.

**Jen** and **Kris**:
The grown-ups of the family.
_Avoid_: Mum, Dad (as names)

**Eva**:
Kris's daughter, who lives at Good Wood half of the time.

**Bobbi** and **Suki**:
The family's two dogs, both Bobby Nuts's friends from the start.

**Ron (Smallie)** and **Reg (Biggie)**:
The family's two cats, called Smallie and Biggie in the stories. They spook Bobby Nuts at first, then become his friends.

**Midnight**:
The family's rabbit.

## Good Wood

**Good Wood**:
The family's name for the real wooded valley where the stories happen, with the Squirrel Hide near the top and the house further down. Every place in the stories is a real place in Good Wood.
_Avoid_: the valley (as a proper name), the forest

**The Burn**:
The tree-lined stream running the whole length of Good Wood. Bobby Nuts follows it down, so it links every Story.
_Avoid_: stream, river, brook

**Walking Path**:
The path that follows the Burn through the trees in lower Good Wood.

**Moor**:
The open high ground above the Pine Forest.

**Pine Forest**:
The mature pines at the very top of Good Wood, with plantations and farmland around them. Bobby Nuts's home, where his journey starts.
_Avoid_: Pine Tops

**Track**:
The way down from the Pine Forest to the Squirrel Hide.
_Avoid_: path (the Walking Path is lower down)

**Squirrel Hide**:
The real wildlife hide up the valley from the house, where the family sits quietly to watch for red squirrels.
_Avoid_: Hut, shelter

**Hide Feeders**:
The nut feeders by the Squirrel Hide. The people who look after the hide take them away for the season, leaving Bobby Nuts hungry and alone, and that is why he sets off down Good Wood.
_Avoid_: Nut Boxes (those are at the house)

**Horse Fields**:
The open fields below the Squirrel Hide where Champion, other horses and cows graze, with buzzards overhead.

**Magic Woods**:
The family's name for where the Burn enters the trees, carpeted in bluebells and wild garlic in spring.
_Avoid_: Bluebell Wood, Magic Wood

**Rock Pools**:
Still pools among the rocks of the Burn.

**Footbridges**:
The arched footbridges where the Walking Path crosses the Burn.

**Rope Swings**:
The rope swings hanging over the Burn.

**Cliffs**:
The rocky cliffs above the Burn in lower Good Wood.

**Spy Station**:
The family's joke name for a building with a huge mast in Good Wood. Bobby Nuts first calls it the Giant Metal Tree.
_Avoid_: Chinese spy station, mast

**Nut Boxes**:
The feeders the family has put up at the house, at the bottom of Good Wood, hoping to tempt red squirrels to come and live there.
_Avoid_: Feeders, Hide Feeders, bird table
