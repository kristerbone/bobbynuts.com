High in the Pine Forest, Bobby Nuts bounced out of his drey.

A drey is a squirrel's nest, round and cosy, high in the branches.

*Boing, boing, boing!* went Bobby Nuts, all the way along the branch.

He was very bouncy. He was also very hungry.

He looked at the pine cone. Empty.

He looked at the next pine cone. Empty.

He looked at the next one. Empty too.

"Oh dear," said Bobby Nuts. His tummy gave a wee rumble. "There's nothing left up here at all."

"Tweet-tweet! Where are you off to?"

A little robin sat on the branch beside him, with a round red chest like a berry.

"I don't know," said Bobby Nuts. "Somewhere with nuts."

"Then follow the Track down," said the robin. "It's a long way, but the wood is kind to those who keep going."

Bobby Nuts looked down the Track. It was very long. It was very quiet. It was a wee bit scary.

Then he felt something in his paw. A single hazelnut, tucked in a crack in the bark. He'd forgotten all about it!

He looked at the nut. Then he looked at the robin.

"Would you like half?" asked Bobby Nuts.

The robin's eyes went round with surprise. "Oh, thank you!"

They shared the nut, crunch by crunch, until it was all gone.

"Now I feel brave," said Bobby Nuts.

"Boing, boing, boing!" said the robin, and she bounced along beside him to the end of the pine trees.

"I'll be here when you come back," she called.

So Bobby Nuts bounced off down the Track, with a full tummy and a new friend.

*Boing, boing, boing!*

And somewhere far below, a warm light was waiting.
