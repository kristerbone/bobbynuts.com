High at the very top of the valley stood a tall, tall pine tree. And in the pine tree, in a cosy nest of leaves and moss, lived a little red squirrel called Bobby Nut.

Bobby had a bushy tail, twitchy ears and a tummy that was always a little bit hungry.

One windy morning, Bobby woke up and went to find his breakfast. He looked under the big root. No nuts. He looked in the old stump. No nuts. He looked behind the mossy stone. Still no nuts!

"Nuts and acorns!" said Bobby. "I know I buried them somewhere!"

The wind went *whoosh*. The pine tree went *creak*. And Bobby climbed to the very top branch to see if there was anything to eat.

From up there, he could see the whole valley. He could see the silver stream, shining like a ribbon. He could see the green woods, soft as a blanket. And far, far down at the bottom, he could see something else.

A tiny golden light.

"What is that?" whispered Bobby.

A small brown bird landed on the branch next to him. It was Pip the Wren, who was tiny, loud and very bossy.

"That," said Pip, "is a house. And do you see those trees around it? Those are hazel trees. They are FULL of hazelnuts."

Bobby's eyes went wide. "Hazelnuts? Real ones? Lots and lots?"

"Lots and lots," said Pip. "But it's a long way down, little squirrel. Past the wood, over the stream, across the meadow. Far too far for you."

Bobby looked at the golden light. He looked at his empty paws. He felt the cold wind in his fur.

Then his ears stopped twitching, and his tail went straight up.

"Tail up!" said Bobby. "I'm going to find that house. And I'm going to live there."

"You? On your own?" said Pip.

"Not on my own," said Bobby, smiling. "I'll make friends on the way."

Pip fluffed up her feathers. "Well," she said, "somebody will have to show you the way. Follow me, Bobby Nut. And try to keep up!"

So Bobby packed one small acorn for the journey, which was all he could find. He said goodbye to his pine tree. And with a hop, a skip and a great big leap, the adventure began.
