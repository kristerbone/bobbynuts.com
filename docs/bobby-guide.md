# Bobby Nut: character and valley guide

Keep every story consistent by checking this guide first. Items marked **TODO** are for you to fill in with real details from your valley and house.

## Bobby
- Young red squirrel, bushy russet tail, cream tummy, ear tufts that twitch when he's thinking.
- Brave but forgetful: he always forgets where he buried his nuts, which causes small problems that lead to adventures.
- Catchphrase: "Nuts and acorns!" (said when surprised). When he has a good idea: "Tail up!"
- Lives at the top of the valley in a cosy drey (leafy nest) in a tall pine.
- Big goal: move down the valley to the house with the garden trees.
- Kind to everyone; solves problems by being clever and asking friends for help, never by being unkind.

## The journey (top to bottom)
1. **The Pine Tops**: windy, home. Tall pines, scattered nuts.
2. **The Bluebell Wood**: cool and shady, full of friends.
3. **The Wobbly Bridge / the stream**: a fallen log over the tumbling water.
4. **The Stone Wall Meadow**: long grass, old walls, hedgehogs and field mice.
5. **The House and Garden** (journey's end): hazel tree, bird table, a warm lit window. **TODO**: add real garden details (a gate colour, a favourite tree, the family's names).

## Friends and neighbours (starter cast, change freely)
- **Pip the Wren**: tiny, loud, bossy, a very good guide.
- **Old Mossy the Badger**: slow, wise, gives advice at dusk.
- **Hattie Hedgehog**: loves rhymes and always carries a spare leaf.
- **Daisy the Fieldmouse**: nervous but generous, knows every hidey-hole.
- **Mr Jay**: a showy bird who steals nuts, but turns out to be lonely. (Gentle "baddie.")

## Tone and style
- Mixed family audience: short sentences and repeated phrases for little ones, a few bigger words for older readers.
- Gentle peril only; every story ends safe and warm.
- Each story: one problem, one clever idea, one kind act.
- British English. 400 to 700 words per story (3 to 5 minute read).

## Adding a story
