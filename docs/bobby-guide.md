# Bobby Nuts: series guide

Read this and [`CONTEXT.md`](../CONTEXT.md) before writing any Story. `CONTEXT.md` holds every name (characters, family, places). This guide holds the decisions about tone, structure and the series plan.

## The premise

Bobby Nuts is a fictional red squirrel, a mix of the real red squirrels the family has seen from the Squirrel Hide. When the Hide Feeders are taken away for the season, he's hungry and alone. He sets off down Good Wood, following the Burn, to the family's house and the Nut Boxes. Along the way he makes friends, and everyone helps each other.

Something has really been eating from the family's Nut Boxes. In the series, that mystery is answered by the Nut Box Squirrel.

## Themes

- **Friendship and sharing**: each Story, Bobby Nuts makes one new friend and shares something good with them.
- **Helping each other**: the family help Bobby Nuts on his journey, and he solves their Nut Box mystery.
- **Being still**: anyone quiet and patient enough can hear the animals talk. Bobby Nuts is very bouncy, and his arc across the series is learning to be still when it matters.

## Rules of the world

- The animals talk and have personalities.
- People can understand and talk to animals only when they are quiet and patient enough. This is why red squirrels are so rarely seen.
- Every place is a real place in Good Wood. Invented things can happen there, but there are no invented places.
- The location is only ever "a wood in Scotland". Never name the nearby town, or anything else that leads to the house. First names only, no surnames.
- No grey squirrels, and no traps.
- The stories can run ahead of real life.

## Who's who in the story

- **The family** (Jen, Kris, Eva) meet Bobby Nuts at the Squirrel Hide. They explain where the Hide Feeders went, invite him down, and ask him to find out who's eating from the Nut Boxes. After that they appear in some Stories, not all. Eva appears most.
- **Pets**: the dogs Bobbi and Suki are friends from the start. The cats, called Smallie and Biggie in the stories, spook Bobby Nuts at first, then become friends. Midnight the rabbit lives at the house.
- **Friends** stay where they live, cheer Bobby Nuts on as he leaves, and all come back for the celebration at the end.
- **Champion** carries Bobby Nuts across the Horse Fields. The buzzard turns out to be friendly.

## Style

- Readers: about ages 3 to 7. Read aloud to younger children and read alone by older ones.
- 400 to 600 words per Story (about 3 to 4 minutes read aloud).
- Short, mostly simple sentences. Dialogue gives each animal its own voice.
- A repeated refrain little ones can join in with.
- One or two big words per Story (e.g. *drey*, *buzzard*), explained by the story itself.
- Short paragraphs that could split into picture pages later.
- A light Scottish flavour: *the Burn*, *wee*, *bonnie*, always clear from context. No full Scots dialect.
- British English. Gentle peril only, and every Story ends safe and warm.

## Formats and publishing

- A **Story** is one chapter, and a **Book** is 4 to 6 Stories in journey order. The books are first chapter books, self-published.
- The website is a drafting space now and becomes a shop window later.
- All illustrations are AI-generated. Declare this when publishing (Amazon KDP asks).

## Series plan

Twelve Stories in three Books, running from autumn to autumn. The order follows the draft route in `CONTEXT.md`. If the route changes, update this plan to match. TODO friends get filled in from real Moments in [`good-wood-notes.md`](good-wood-notes.md).

### Book 1: Autumn into winter, leaving home
1. **Pine Forest**: The Hide Feeders are gone, and Bobby Nuts is hungry and alone. He sets off down the Track. *Friend: TODO*
2. **Squirrel Hide**: He meets Jen, Kris and Eva and has to keep still long enough to hear them. They invite him down and give him the Nut Box mystery. *Friend: the family*
3. **Horse Fields**: Open ground, with a buzzard overhead. Champion carries him across. *Friends: Champion and the buzzard*
4. **Into the Burn**: Winter and the first snow. *Friend: a badger*

### Book 2: Spring into summer, down the Burn
5. **Magic Woods**: Bluebells and wild garlic. *Friend: a deer*
6. **Rock Pools**: *Friend: TODO*
7. **Footbridges**: Eva walks Bobbi and Suki, and Bobby Nuts meets his name-twin. *Friends: the dogs*
8. **Rope Swings**: The family on a summer walk. *Friend: TODO*

### Book 3: Late summer into autumn, home
9. **Cliffs**: The scariest climb, where he has to be still and careful. *Friend: TODO*
10. **Spy Station**: The Giant Metal Tree. *Friend: TODO*
11. **The Garden**: Smallie and Biggie spook him, then they make friends. *Friends: the cats and Midnight*
12. **The Nut Boxes**: He's still enough to find the Nut Box Squirrel, the mystery is solved, and every friend comes to celebrate. *Friend: the Nut Box Squirrel*

## Writing a Story

1. Jen or Kris bring an idea or a real Moment (see [`good-wood-notes.md`](good-wood-notes.md)).
2. Claude drafts it in Bobby Nuts's voice, following this guide.
3. Jen or Kris rewrite it.
4. Save it as `src/content/stories/NN-short-title.md` with front matter `title`, `summary`, `order`, `emoji` and `readingMinutes`, then commit and push. The site rebuilds automatically.
5. Mark the Moments it used in `good-wood-notes.md`.
