### Magic Walk (told by Kris, 7 October 2026)

The loop walk we've done many times, which is how we first found the Squirrel Hide. Dates are not known; these are the things we remember from many walks.

- Magic trees that come to life.
- Great gnarly oaks, perfect for hugging.
- Beech trees 300 to 400 years old, too big to hug, with names etched into them. One is called Ralph because of his huge engraving.
- Techno Woods: exceptionally tall pines that worked well with the music we played on the JBL at the time.
- Techno Cows and Sheep: they loved our music too and followed us along the fence lines.
- Spiky gorse that smells of coconut.
- Heather on the "moor" leading up to the trig point, with a view all the way back down the valley.
- The return loop past "the pub".
- Suki with us every time, and visitors visiting.

### Magic Walk route, outbound (told by Kris, 8 October 2026)

The landmarks in order, about 4 km as far as the Squirrel Hide. More to add later.

1. Good Wood, Mid Wood, Hollywood, Dam Burst Valley, a farmer's field and the forester's cottage.
2. Enter the Magic Woods. The path zigzags up and down the stream bank, sometimes high (the Rope Swings), sometimes low at the stream. Along the way: rock cliffs, Rock Pools, the stream cascading over rocks, fallen trees, and a small stone arched bridge with the stream running under it. All of this is the Magic Woods.
3. Exit the Magic Woods at the Spy Station, then rise up a farm track to the private road.
4. The private road leads up to Champion, his sad friend and his white stallion friends.
5. Reach the Squirrel Hide, about 4 km in.
6. More fields, then a woodland walk, which is where Good Wood got its name. Ancient forest sits right next to plantation. The plantation has no life at ground level and no mycelium networks, and is stark. The ancient forest is the opposite.
